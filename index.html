Crescendo
by NVRR

Your music, beautifully played.

Crescendo is an offline music player for Android. It plays the songs already on your phone, with a glassy, smooth design and serious sound controls.

  • 10-band equalizer with presets, bass, treble, stereo width and a limiter
  • Crescendo Spatial: 3D sound modes for headphones (3D Headphones, Wide Stage, Studio, Concert Hall, Surround)
  • Synced, word-by-word lyrics from your files, with optional online lookup
  • Spectrum and waveform visualizers that take their colours from the album art
  • Albums grouped by folder, A–Z fast scroll, playlists, sleep timer
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking
