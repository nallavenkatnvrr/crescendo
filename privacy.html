Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026 · App: Crescendo (com.nvrr.crescendo) · Developer: NVRR (Venkat Nalla), Hyderabad, India

In short: Crescendo plays the music files already on your phone. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal data. The only time the app uses the internet is to look up song lyrics, and only if you turn that on.

1. Music on your device

With your permission (“Music and audio” on Android 13 and later, or “Storage” on older versions), Crescendo reads the audio files on your phone and their tags (title, artist, album, cover art and embedded lyrics) so it can build your library and play your music. All of this happens on your device. Your music files and your library are never uploaded anywhere.

2. Online lyrics (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Find lyrics online in Settings, or tap Search Online on a song, Crescendo sends that song’s title, artist, album name and length to LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free, community-run lyrics database, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. LRCLIB returns the lyrics, which Crescendo saves on your device so it doesn’t have to ask again.

Like any website, LRCLIB receives your device’s IP address when the app contacts it. LRCLIB is an independent service with its own practices; the developer of Crescendo does not receive or keep this information. Lyrics are provided by LRCLIB’s community and belong to their respective rights holders.

Crescendo does not contact any other online service. It does not download album covers from the internet, and it does not use Apple, Google or any other music catalogue.

3. Notifications and background playback

Crescendo asks for permission to show notifications so it can display playback controls while music plays in the background and on your lock screen. It does not send you promotional notifications.

4. Things you choose to share

  • Share Song Link creates a YouTube Music search link for the song and opens Android’s share menu. Nothing is sent until you pick an app and send it yourself.
  • Crash reports: if the app ever closes unexpectedly, a short error report is kept on your device. It is only sent if you tap Share Report and choose how to send it.

5. Data stored on your device

Your settings, playlists, favourites, play counts, equalizer presets and saved lyrics are stored only inside the app on your phone. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app. There is no account and nothing is stored on any server run by the developer.

6. What Crescendo does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising ID
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with anyone

7. Children

Crescendo is a general music player and is not directed at children under 13. Lyrics retrieved online come from a public database and are not filtered.

8. Your rights

Because the developer does not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. This applies under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws elsewhere. If you have any questions, you can contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
